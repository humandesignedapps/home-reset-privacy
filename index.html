Home Reset Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11/20/2026

Home Reset is a home tidying app published by Sergio A. Mercado/Human Designed Apps ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

The short version

Home Reset has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking. Your spaces, tasks and session history stay on your phone. The only information that leaves your phone is what is needed to confirm a Home Reset Pro subscription.

Information stored on your device

The spaces, tasks, session history, reminder settings and preferences you create are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. If you have Android's backup feature turned on, Google may include this data in your device backup under your Google account, according to Google's own privacy policy.

Subscriptions and purchases

Home Reset Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never receive your card details, name, email address or billing address.

To confirm that a subscription is genuine, the app sends the purchase token issued by Google Play to our verification service, which checks it with Google. A purchase token is a code that identifies the purchase and does not by itself identify you. The verification service does not store the token. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard technical request data (such as IP address) to deliver and protect the service.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows a timer notification during a session and an optional daily reminder. These are created on your device and no notification data is sent to us.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or advertising ID. We do not sell or share personal information, and the app contains no third-party advertising or analytics software.

If you contact us

If you email us for support or feedback, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and use it only to reply.

Children

Home Reset is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all app data from your device. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
